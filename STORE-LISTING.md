## Description (4,000 at most)

    You know where Chile is. But can you draw it?

    Outlines is a geography game in two steps. First, work out a mystery country or US state from clues that start vague and get sharper. Then draw its outline from memory, with your finger, on an empty canvas.

    HOW IT WORKS
    • Up to five clues per round, from "It is in the Southern Hemisphere" down to the give-away.
    • Name the place on the first clue for 6 points, on the fifth for 2. Even if you miss it, you still draw it for 1.
    • Then draw. Your accuracy is the multiplier: a 70% drawing turns your points into 70x, a perfect one into 100x.
    • A wrong guess costs a point, so think before you type.
    • At the end, lift the bars one by one and see every place you drew on a 3D globe or a US map.

    TWO PACKS
    • Countries: all 196, with 20 different clues for every one, so the same country reads differently each game.
    • US States: all 50, with capitals, nicknames, neighbours, landmarks and more.
    • Four levels: Beginner, Standard, Expert and Master (every place in the pack).

    PLAY YOUR WAY
    • Solo against one to three bots, each with its own style.
    • Pass and play: two to four people share one phone or iPad, with up to two bots.
    • 5 to 30 rounds, adjustable timers, and a faint outline to practise with.
    • A "Did you know?" fact after every round, and your record against the bot for each pack.

    MADE WITH CARE
    • Haptics, sound and smooth motion; dark and light looks; reduce motion.
    • Thirteen languages: English, Deutsch, Italiano, Español, Français, Português (Brasil), Bahasa Indonesia, 简体中文, हिन्दी, 日本語, 한국어, العربية, ไทย.
    • Works fully offline. No account, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Nothing you do leaves your phone.

## Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

    Outlines is a single-device game. No sign-in, no account, no network: it runs fully offline (please feel free to test in airplane mode).

    To play: tap "Solo", then "Start the game". Read the clue and type a guess (or tap "Next clue"), then draw the place's outline with a finger and tap "Done". After the last round, tap the bars (or "Lift every bar") to see the result.

    Nothing is sold, there are no ads and no in-app purchases.

    Settings → "Record my games" (off by default) keeps a game log on the device only; the player can share it themselves for beta testing. Nothing is sent anywhere.
